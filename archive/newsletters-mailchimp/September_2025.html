Labor Day Newsletter
Brothers,

Happy Labor Day! 

The NEO E-Board would like to wish you all a wonderful day of grilling and spending time with your loved ones. School is back in full swing for both the kids and our undergrad brothers. Here's a quick look at what's coming up. See below for some more details!
  • Wednesday September 17th - Ray's Place Happy Hour starting at 6pm
  • Saturday October 11th - KSU Homecoming and Delta Chi Pig Roast
  • Monday October 13th - Delta Chi Founder's Day Dinner (and NEO Officer Elections)
 More info for the events and other happenings can be found below. We hope to see you there!


ITB
 
DAMN PROUD!

Recently, we were recognized by Delta Chi HQ for outstanding alumni chapter programming. Each of you has helped us achieve this in some way, whether as an officer on the executive board, providing feedback on messages and events, or simply by being a member of the chapter. We couldn't have done this without you! Here's to another great year!

Cleveland Whiskey Tour
Last month, brothers got together to enjoy a private tour of the Cleveland Whiskey Distillery. During this tour, they learned about the history of the distillery, including it's start-up as an incubator at CSU and observed the final flavoring and bottling done onsite at the distillery. 

Of course, they also got to taste a wide variety of flavors from the distillery. Keep an eye out for Cleveland Whiskey next time you're in the area, it's primed to become a favorite location in the future with the inclusion of a food and tasting room planned for the future!

Ray's Place Happy Hour
Come out to Ray's Place in Kent on Wednesday, September 17th, at 6 pm for our annual Ray's Place happy hour! This annual tradition is a great opportunity to bond with alumni, the undergrads, and their PNMs. Stories, drinks, and laughs are always share,d and every year the event gets bigger and bigger! We can't wait to see you there!

Upcoming Events:

  • KSU Homecoming 2025! 
    • The time of year is quickly approaching. Mark your calendars for Saturday, October 11th, for our KSU Homecoming Pig Roast and Tailgate! This event is one of our biggest and it's always a fan favorite! Bring your families to experience fellowship and great food! Stay tuned for more info!
 
  • Delta Chi Founder's Day
    • After homecoming, our Founder's Day is the following Monday, October 13th. This tradition is a great time to join together to reflect on our experiences and look forward to a new and growing year. More info will be coming on where our dinner will be held.  
    • Additionally, we host our NEO E-Board Officer Elections during this event. If you're interested in holding a position and making a change, this is your chance!
