Consider Joining The NEO Alumni Chapter Exec Board!
The chapter has recently filled the open "B" officer role, but is actively looking to hold elections for the open "E" officer role. The "E" manages recruitment for the NEO Alumni Chapter and actively participates in monthly chapter meetings and executive board discussions / planning sessions.

Miscellaneous Items To Be Aware Of...
  • Dale Carnegie Training: Disagree Agreeably Virtual Workshop 
    • Register to join Delta Chi brothers on Thursday, October 5th from 2-3:30pm EST for Dale Carnegie's highly acclaimed "Disagree Agreeably" virtual workshop.

      This virtual workshop will provide active and alumni members insights into their own personality and reactions when dealing with differences of opinion surrounding our “hot buttons.” We will learn to control our emotions, give others the benefit of the doubt, and to express our opinions in ways that allow for acceptance, agreeable outcomes, and improved productivity. 

      All members (actives and alumni) are welcome to register for this virtual interactive opportunity. Seats are limited to 100 Delta Chis. Register now to reserve your seat!
 
  • FREE DIGITAL HEADSHOTS: There may be an opportunity for our group to participate in having free professional head shots taken. As we learn more about the event, we'll provide an update to the group. MORE TO COME....
 
  • FINALLY, local chapters are in need of filling E-Board/ABT positions. Specifically, the Case Western chapter is looking for a new ABT President and the Kent State chapter is looking to fill the role of  'BB'. Also, Kent State will be in need of a new Faculty Advisor at the end of the school year as their current advisor will be retiring. If you have interest in taking on a position, or happen to know a Kent State faculty/staff member that you'd think might be ideal in an advisory position, please contact our alumni chapter so we can help pass along the updates to the proper parties. Thank you for your help!
