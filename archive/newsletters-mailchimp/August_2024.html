August 2024 Newsletter / Convention Recap
Brothers,

So much has happened since the last newsletter. We had a fun happy hour as we celebrated Xmas in July at the Wild Eagle Saloon. Then we rolled right into the Miami Convention at the end of the month. 

Convention was amazing. We had a great group of 7 brothers and extended family attend this time around. Our alumni chapter bagged 3 convention awards and we're in the running for the Chicago Cup. It was an amazing outcome, but still shows we have more to work towards so that should keep us motivated. 

As a group, we had a great bit of fun touring a variety of spots in the greater south beach area of Miami. We toured a variety of breweries via a party bus, saw some of the public waterways and beaches, toured the Everglades, visited some awesome boroughs and enjoyed much of the food Miami had to offer. We were even able to escape town just before the storm!

It was easy to say that convention was a success and we look forward to the next one in Indy. A large group of us is already discussing the plans so please consider, especially if you've never been. With it being in the Midwest, this is the perfect opportunity to test the waters of attending a convention without a major logistical commitment. Please consider and you won't regret it. 

Again, the alumni events this year have been great and we're looking for more Brothers to join in the fun!!  

Next up is the Kent Rays Happy Hour (9/18 - 6 PM)!
