Sept 2022 Newsletter / Founders' Day
Join us for our Annual Delta Chi Founders' Day Dinner.  We will be meeting at R Shea Brewing in Akron this October 15th to Celebrate the Founding of Delta Chi and our Alumni Chapter which started 6 years ago in 2016 on Founders' Day.  This year's event will be from 5:30pm - 9:30pm in the party room.  Cost is $20 for Non-members $10 for Members and Free for Undergrads.  All are welcome. Come out and celebrate the Brotherhood!

This Event also serves as our Annual meeting to elect officers and reset of our fiscal dues calendar, so renew your dues and get discounts on events as a member today.  Visit our Event RSVP site to book today!
We have been busy since our last newsletter
In early August the Brothers of the Alumni Chapter supported the Children's Hunger Alliance at their fundraiser in Cleveland at Punch Bowl Social.  Members donated items to be auctioned and came out and supported the cause through donations and a great night in downtown Cleveland.
In early September the Alumni Chapter hosted it's Third and final Summer happy Hour.  Seeing some new faces join the group.  We hope to continue to see and meet new Alumni in the Area to join our events.  Or come back if we haven't seen you in a while. 
If you would like to run for one of our Chapter offices? Elections are held at our Founders Day Meeting yearly.  Position of A, B, C, D and E will be up for election, please reach out to neodxalumni@gmail.com for more info. Must be a member to serve in office.
Make sure to follow-us on Social Media links on bottom of newsletter. Or at neodeltachi.com
