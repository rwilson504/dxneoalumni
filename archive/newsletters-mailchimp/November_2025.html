November Newsletter
Brothers,

Happy November!

2025 is wrapping up fast as we enter our second to last month of the year! Take a look below for a recap of the last month, big news and upcoming events!

ITB
 

October Recap 

KSU Homecoming
 
The undergrads had a great time hosting the annual Delta Chi Pig Roast at this year's KSU homecoming! It was great to meet all the new recently initiated members, potential new members, and getting to see the actives and alumni we've already established bonds with. Thank you to all who came out, we're already looking forward to next year!

135th Founder's Day
The Alumni Chapter and the Kent State undergrads came together to celebrate our 135th Founders' Day at Barrio in Kent. Alumni brothers got to reconnect with the undergrad brothers and meet the new AMs from the Kent State Chapter. After dinner, we returned to the official chapter house to participate in Kent State Founder's Day traditions including the rock painting ceremony and other special events back at the house. 

We had a great time seeing everyone and celebrating our legacy as a brotherhood!

A New NEO Eboard!
At our Founder's Day dinner, we held nominations and elections for E-Board members for the 2026 Fiscal Year. Please help us welcome our new (and returning) NEO Officers:
 
"A" Brian Cain - Kent State '01
"B" Daniel Russell - Kent State '03
"C" Kenny Strait - Kent State '24
"D" Justin (J.D) Donnelly - Kent State '02
"E" Geoff Westerfield - Ohio State '01

Thank you, gentlemen, for your dedication to this chapter. We look forward to seeing what you do this year! Additionally, we'd like to take this time to thank our previous "A", Michael Lippy, for his previous service to the NEO Alumni Chapter. Thank you, Lippy, for all you've done, you can rest easy knowing the chapter you led will be in good hands. 

Up Next!

Join us for the annual Holiday Get Together on Wednesday Dec. 17th 6pm at Wild Eagle Saloon.  Don't forget your ugly sweater and bring a gift to participate in the white elephant exchange!
