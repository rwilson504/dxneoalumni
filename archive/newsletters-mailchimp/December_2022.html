December Newsletter
The Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter is inviting all Delta Chis in the area to join us for our Holiday Get Together.  This is a time to meet up in Brotherhood to celebrate together the Holiday Season again after a year awhile from each one.  This is open to all Delta Chis Alumni, from any Chapter and any era.
The cost is free.  Food and Drink are on your own.  Starting at 6pm at the Wild Eagle Saloon, 5001 E Royalton Rd, Broadview Heights, OH 44147.  
Just look for the Delta Chi On the Table and find your Brothers!!
Brewery Tour Recap:  On October 30th, Eight Brothers from the Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter chartered a tour bus to tour and sample the best craft beer Akron/ Canton had to offer.  The tour started at 10:30am with the Brothers meeting at Fat Head's Brewery in Canton.
We headed north to Akron and started our tour at 11am and Lock 15.  Over the next 8 hours we traveled up and down I77 hitting 7 unique great breweries that the area had to offer.  A special thanks must go out to Brother Dan Russell, Kent State '03.  Dan did a month of planning to ensure our event went off without a hitch.  Every Brewery knew we were coming and had tables reserved.
Make sure to join us next year as we are in the works on planning our next Brewery tour.
