June 2022 Newsletter / Summer Happy Hours / Convention Recap
Don't miss out on our last Happy Hour of the Summer Sept. 1st.  Mark your Calendar today!!!  The cost is free.  Food and Drink are on your own.
Our Next Happy Hour will start at 5:30pm at Slyman's Tavern at 6901 Rockside Rd. Independence, OH 44131
Just look for the Delta Chi On the Table and find your Brothers!!
This past weekend 9 of our members traveled to St. Louis to attend the 62nd International Delta Chi Convention.  After 8 hours of driving the Brothers Arrived in St. Louis for a weekend of fun, bonding and supporting our Chapter "D" Justin (J.D.) Donnelly on his run for "DD".  The Members were able to get out of the hotel and take in the sites around St. Louis.

 
Our Alumni Chapter teamed up with the Detroit / Windsor Motor Cities Alumni Chapter for a Trolley ride Craft Beer Tour taking in 4 of St. Louis' finest.  Members also, went to and visited the Arch and with the Convention was hosted by the St. Louis Cardinals for a reception on the warning track of the field.  Able to participate in batting cages, pitching from in the bullpens and take pictures with the World Series Trophy.  As always we hope to attend the next convention in 2024 in Ft. Lauderdale Florida with even more members from the Alumni Chapter.  As we have always had one of the Largest delegation at Convention from year to year.  Join the Chapter Today and come with us in two years!!!
We also would like to announce that our Chapter "B" Michael Lippy will now be moving up to the position of Chapter "A".  We would like to thank Steve Bossart our outgoing "A" for all his time he has been  serving the Alumni Chapter over the years and stepping back in as "A".

If you would like to run for one of our Chapter offices? Elections are held at our Founders Day Meeting yearly. If you want to fill our vacant "B" or "C" positions prior to elections please reach out to neodxalumni@gmail.com for consideration. Must be a member to serve in office.
Make sure to follow-us on Social Media links on bottom of newsletter. Or at neodeltachi.com
