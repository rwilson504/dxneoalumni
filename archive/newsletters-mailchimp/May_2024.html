2024 Continues To Keep Us Busy...
Brothers,

We've been busy so far this year! Just in the past week we took part in the Children's Hunger Alliance fundraiser. To clarify the Children’s Hunger Alliance is a statewide nonprofit organization dedicated to ending childhood hunger. In attendance were 5 brothers and some significant others. Brother Doug Tayek donated 2 professional photos for auction which raised over $375. The brothers in attendance contributed another $560 making it a very successful contribution. 

We also held a brotherhood event at Top Golf Cleveland this past weekend which was a great time. We'll plan to do another Top Golf outing later this year (likely in the Fall) so please stay tuned.

Next up is the planned Guardians baseball game in June. We look forward to seeing you there.
