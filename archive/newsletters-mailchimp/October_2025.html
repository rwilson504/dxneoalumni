135 Years of Brotherhood
Brothers,

Happy October!

As you all know, October is a very important month for Delta Chi, and this year is no different! In fact, events have lined up so well that our two annual traditions are lining up on the same weekend! Check it out below. We hope to see you and your loved ones soon!

ITB
 
11 October - KSU Homecoming!

Get excited for our annual KSU Homecoming Pig Roast on October 11th! As in recent years, we'll be hosting the event in the parking lot of Dix Stadium to allow for more space and connect with the community. Bring your family and friends along too! 

Homecoming is always a packed event, so be prepared for busy traffic around the stadium. Paid parking at Dix typically starts around 9 am; active brothers will be prepping the pig around 3 am, so don't worry about arriving too early. We plan to have the pig ready around 12 pm, and you are more than welcome to bring extra food/snacks as well, but there is no obligation!

Finally, if you are in the giving spirit, the undergraduates are open for donations to help offset the cost of the pig. Donations can be sent via venmo to the "B" Chris Luke - @Christopher-Luke-53. Any amount is greatly appreciated!

13 October - Founder's Day Dinner


The Monday following Homecoming, we'll be celebrating 135 years of Delta Chi during our founder's day dinner. Join us at Barrio in Kent at 6pm for dinner followed by events rooted in Kent State Delta Chi tradition at the Chapter House located on 222 University Drive. 

Additionally, we'll be hosting our nominations and elections for lettered positions for the NEO E-Board. We'll be looking to fill the role of "A" along with other positions.
