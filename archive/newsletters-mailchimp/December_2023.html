Closing Out A Great 2023!

Happy Holidays Brothers!

November was another successful month for brotherhood activities. We had a nice Collision Bend brewery meet-up and online social with each having strong participation. Thanks to all that attended.

We look forward to closing out the year with our Holiday Celebration at the Wild Eagle Saloon. Let's finish the year on a good note by having a great event.

If we don't see you, be safe and have a wonderful holiday season!

Miscellaneous Items To Be Aware Of...

BEER TOUR UPDATE - Postponed to 2/24/2024..... Per timing and other logistical factors, it made sense for the chapter to postpone the event until 2024. The location will be Cleveland instead of Columbus and more info will be provided as we look to iron out the details....

CHALLENGE COINS: Reminder that the coins are available. They will be provided to all new / renewed / virtual members and will be available for shipping or pick up at any of our upcoming events. The coins will also be available to purchase for non-members. Cost is $12.50 per coin. To purchase please go here... http://legfi.com/app/merchandise/4264
 

FREE DIGITAL HEADSHOTS FUNDRAISER: There may be an opportunity for our group to participate in having discounted ($5-10) professional head shots taken. We look to partner up with one of our area undergrad chapters and the opportunity will be open to all. The proceeds will support the V-Foundation! and their cancer research. MORE TO COME....

FINALLY, Congratulations to our Brother Chuck Bonacci for winning his Twinsburg City Council election. We are proud of you and wish you nothing but success!
