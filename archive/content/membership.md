## 2026 Alumni Chapter Members

Becoming a member is about more than staying connected—it’s about reigniting the bond of brotherhood that first brought us together. Membership creates opportunities to meet and build relationships with brothers from other chapters, expand your personal and professional network, and stay engaged with the broader vision of our organization. From meaningful workshops—such as leadership development, career growth, and mentorship roundtables—to powerful networking events and our annual Founder’s Day celebration, membership keeps you inspired, involved, and growing.  
 

Joining also means continuing to give back to the chapters and traditions that shaped us. Your $35 annual membership (or $15 for virtual members)helps strengthen local programming, enhance chapter collaboration, and create welcoming, high-quality experiences for brothers at every stage of life. Most importantly, it keeps our shared legacy strong—ensuring that the spirit of brotherhood, service, and connection continues to thrive for years to come.
