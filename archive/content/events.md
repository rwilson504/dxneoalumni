###### October 13, 2017: Founder's Day Dinner

Our 2nd annual Northeast Ohio all Chapter Founder Day Dinner.  Sponsered by the Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter, featuring the Case Western Chapter and Kent State Colony.  This year's event was at the Weymouth Country Club in Medina. See pictures in our [photo gallery](https://dxneoalumni.wixsite.com/main/photo-gallery)

###### May 16, 2017: A night at the ballpark

The Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter sponsored a night to see your 2016 American League Champion Cleveland Indians take on the Tampa Bay Rays at Progressive Field.  The event saw Alumni from five different Chapters and their families, came out to meet and enjoy Brotherhood!! See pictures in our [photo gallery](https://dxneoalumni.wixsite.com/main/photo-gallery) 

###### October 13, 2016: Founder's Day Dinner

The Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter, co-sponsored with the Case Western Reserve Chapter a celebration of our Founder's Day with a Dinner at Harry's Steakhouse in Independence, OH.  This event mark the reformation of the Northeast Ohio Alumni Chapter.  See pictures in our [photo gallery](https://dxneoalumni.wixsite.com/main/photo-gallery)
